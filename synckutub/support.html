Support

Something not working, or a question? The best way to reach us is a short note on our public support page:

Write to support

You need a free GitHub account to post. Please do not include private file contents or passwords.

Please tell us

  • Your SyncKutub version (shown at the top of the window).
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Roughly how big your folder is (number of files, scanned or not).

Common questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC".

Click More info, then Run anyway. This warning appears for new apps from small publishers.

The app says my computer does not have enough memory.

SyncKutub needs at least 12 GB of memory (16 GB recommended).

My big folder is still being read.

Large folders and scanned documents take time. Files become searchable one by one while it works; the Sources panel shows progress.

Where is the latest version?

Latest release. The app also updates itself.
