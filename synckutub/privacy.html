Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The short version

SyncKutub works on your own computer. We do not run any servers that receive your files, questions or answers. We do not collect analytics, accounts or advertising data.

What stays on your computer

  • The files in the folders you choose are read where they are. Text extracted from them, the search index and your saved chats are stored in a folder on your computer.
  • Reading, searching and the built-in local AI model all run on your computer.

What can leave your computer

  • Update checks. The app contacts GitHub (github.com) to see whether a new version exists and to download it. No data from your files is sent.
  • Model downloads. If the app needs to fetch AI model files, it downloads them from their public sources. Nothing from your files is sent.
  • Optional outside AI. Only if you choose "Claude Code" or "Antigravity" as the answering tool, the short passages relevant to your question, and the question itself, are sent to that tool, which uses Anthropic's or Google's service under your own sign-in. Their handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policies. The app shows a note beside the question box when this is on. With the default local model, nothing is sent.
  • Optional connections. If you switch on "Let AI tools use my files", tools on your computer that you connect can ask SyncKutub for passages. That stays on your computer unless the tool itself sends them on.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app and deleting its app folder removes the index, extracted text and chats. SyncKutub never changes or deletes your original files.

Children

SyncKutub is a general tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes. Questions: see Support.
