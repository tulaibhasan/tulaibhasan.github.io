SyncKutub reads the folders you choose (PDFs, scans, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, pictures) and answers in plain language. Every answer points to the exact page, and clicking it opens your original file at that spot.

Download for Windows
Free · Windows 10/11, 64-bit · needs 12 GB of memory or more (16 GB recommended)

Private by default

Reading, searching and answering run on your own computer. Your files are not uploaded anywhere.

Every claim is checkable

Answers come with clickable citations that open the original page, highlighted.

Folders, not uploads

Pick a folder once. New and changed files are picked up in the background.

Reads scans too

Scanned pages, tables and pictures are read with built-in text recognition, English and Urdu.

Big folders welcome

Built for contracts, BOQs and bid documents with thousands of pages. A large PDF is searchable within about a minute.

Your choice of AI

Answer with the built-in local model, or optionally with your own Claude Code or Antigravity sign-in.

How to install

  1. Click Download for Windows, then under Assets choose the file ending in -setup.exe.
  2. Open the downloaded file.
  3. Windows may show a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC". This appears for new apps from small publishers. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. Follow the installer.
The app updates itself: when a new version is out, it installs the next time you open it.

Good to know

  • Needs at least 12 GB of memory. With 12 to 16 GB, answers are slower.
  • The first time you add a big folder, reading everything takes a while. It works in the background, and files become searchable one by one.
