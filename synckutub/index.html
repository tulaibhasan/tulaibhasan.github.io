How it works

Point it at a folder. Ask anything.

No uploading, no setup, no accounts. SyncKutub works quietly in the background and keeps up as your files change.

1

Choose your folders

Pick the folders that hold your contracts, reports and drawings. Your files stay where they are.

2

It reads everything

Typed pages, scans, tables and pictures are all read, in English and Urdu. Large PDFs become searchable in about a minute.

3

Ask and verify

Get a clear answer with citations. One click opens the original page with the passage highlighted.

Built for real work

Made for big folders and hard documents.

Designed first for engineers and contract managers who live in thousands of pages.

✓

Every claim is checkable

Answers cite the exact page. If something is not in your files, SyncKutub says so instead of guessing.

☷

Reads scans and tables

Built-in text recognition handles scanned pages, columns, and Urdu. Difficult pages get a slower, more careful second read.

☰

Spreadsheets too

Ask about BOQs and estimates, including totals and counts, with the answer traced back to the sheet.

↻

Always in sync

New and changed files are picked up automatically. Copies of the same file are recognised and not read twice.

★

Your choice of AI

Answer with the built-in local model, or optionally with your own Claude Code or Antigravity sign-in for stronger answers.

⇩

Updates itself

New versions install automatically the next time you open the app.

Private by default

Your files never leave your computer.

Reading, searching and answering with the built-in model all happen on your machine. There are no accounts, no uploads and no analytics.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. SyncKutub is free to download and use.

What computer do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with at least 12 GB of memory. 16 GB or more is recommended; with 12 to 16 GB, answers are slower.

Windows shows "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

This warning appears for new apps from small publishers. Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer is published openly on GitHub.

How long does a big folder take?

It works in the background and files become searchable one by one. A large PDF is searchable by words in about a minute; scanned and image-heavy folders take longer.

Do my files get uploaded?

No. Only if you choose Claude Code or Antigravity as the answering tool are the short passages for your question sent to that service, under your own sign-in. The app tells you when this is on.
